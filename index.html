Who We Are

A family mill, run like a precision workshop.

Vandana Industries began as a single stone mill in Khajuri Karodi and has grown into a full-line rice and wheat processing unit — without losing the hands-on care that started it. Every batch is watched, tasted and graded before it leaves the floor.

We work directly with regional farmers, which means shorter supply chains, fresher grain, and prices that stay honest for everyone.

Farm-direct sourcing

Grain bought straight from regional growers.

Modern grading line

Sortex-cleaned, moisture-tested batches.

Bulk & retail packing

From 1kg pouches to 50kg export bags.

Pan-India dispatch

Reliable logistics to 15+ states.

How It's Milled

Six stages, one standard.

Every sack that leaves Vandana Industries has passed through the same disciplined line — from raw paddy to a grain you can trust.

01

Procurement

Paddy & wheat sourced fresh from partner farms nearby.

02

Cleaning

Stones, husk & dust removed on the pre-cleaning line.

03

Milling

Precision hulling & grinding tuned for each grain type.

04

Polishing

Gentle polishing for grain that cooks even & clean.

05

Grading

Sortex sorting by size, colour and moisture content.

06

Packing

Sealed & labelled — from 1kg retail to bulk export bags.
